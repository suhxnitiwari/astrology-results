                    How the planets talk

                    The Aspects

                    Aspects are the angles between planets. Lines in aqua flow easily, lines in rose create friction that pushes growth, and gold marks planets fused together.

                    Minor aspects

                    The fine print

                    Minor aspects use tighter orbs and are weighed less, and not every astrologer uses them. Arrows show whether an aspect was applying (still tightening) or separating at birth, a distinction that matters most in traditional astrology.
